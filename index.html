The problem

A study conducted by Duplo in the year 2025 showed that in Nigeria, SMEs lost 5k - 10k USD in every 100k USD they transacted across Nigerian borders in one year. More money is spent on transport to the banks to make the transaction and the time away from their business also loses them a significant amount of money.

By 2030, we hope to change this by utilizing the wider regional integration and the improvement of digital financial infrastructure in place to ensure that the SMEs and anyone sending money across Nigerian borders incurs less cost, 700 - 2k USD for every 100k USD transacted in a year, and is able to do it from their mobile phone without needing to visit the bank.

What it takes to send $100,000 annually

  • Amount transacted · $100,000 (83.3%)The money actually sent
  • Transaction fee · $10,000 (8.3%)10% of the amount
  • Transport fee · $5,000 (4.2%)5% of the amount
  • Opportunity cost · $5,000 (4.2%)5% of the amount

To send $100,000 today, an SME spends about $120,000 in total: the amount itself plus a 10% fee, 5% for transport and 5% in opportunity cost.

Our solution: cross-border payments inside Opay

We will partner with an existing fintech and microfinance provider such as Opay, which today operates only locally. The partnership lets the platform host cross-border transactions alongside the trusted local transactions people already make.

NTKT will implement everything international transactions require, while Opay hosts the feature for its users. All 45 million Opay users, mainly SMEs, can use it as long as they have an active Opay account. They can send money across borders from their business or home, without installing a new app.

See it in action

A working prototype of the feature inside the Opay app. Log in with the prefilled test account, open Send Abroad, choose a country and send.

  • Inside the app they already use

    Send Abroad sits next to Airtime, Data and TV on the Opay home screen. Opay is at the top left and NTKT at the top right.

  • See what they get before you pay

    Type an amount in Naira, pick Togo, Tanzania, Kenya or Benin, and the local-currency amount appears instantly.

  • Confirmed with the Opay PIN

    Payment only goes through after the user enters their Opay PIN.
